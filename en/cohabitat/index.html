FAQ

What people often ask us.

Do my tenants have to pay?

No subscription. The plan is paid by the owner. Tenants only pay for what they book (a space, a vehicle, a greenhouse plot) at the rates you set, which covers the cost of shared resources.

Do residents need to install anything?

No. CoHabitat opens in the browser and can be added to the phone's home screen in one tap. An Android app is also available.

Where is our data stored?

Each building has its own database, separate from the others. You can also host everything inside the building, on a dedicated machine, on a closed network.

Is it compliant with Quebec's Law 25?

Yes: explicit consent for analytics, data isolated per building, export and deletion of personal data on request.

Can we turn on only some modules?

Yes. No greenhouse or no vehicles? Modules can be switched off one by one, and the app adapts.
