The concept

A chain,

MaaS — Mobility as a Service
Six modes, one account.

MaaS — Mobility as a Service — means bringing every transport service, public, shared or rented, together into a single offer. At Modulimo that offer has a name: CoHabitat, the app included with your home.

01
Book in one place

Minivans, velomobiles and neighbour carpooling are booked from the same screen. The uses see one another: a velomobile already committed to a carpool trip does not show as free for that slot.

02
Pay from a single balance

No per-service subscription, no card to top up: a single balance, charged by usage time. The cost is shown before you confirm, and a cancellation made more than two hours ahead is refunded.

03
Chain without a break

What belongs to the building lives in the app; public transit stays public and starts at the doorstep. MaaS does not replace the STM — it removes the friction between it and you.

The intercity horizon

The link
that is coming.

Modulimo's inter-modal chain will be connected to the future Quebec-Toronto high-speed train. Just as Hydro-Québec set new standards by raising transmission voltage to 750 kV, the high-speed/high-frequency rail represents an opportunity to redefine intercity mobility in Quebec — and Modulimo will be on that network.
